# Vice & Versa — Chapter One: The Joy Plant — script v2 changes

These are the edits from v1. Everything else in the script is unchanged.
**Check each one against the book before recording.** The script's rule is "every fact comes from
Chapter One as printed", so where the book has the same error, fix the book too.

| # | Where | v1 | v2 | Why |
|---|-------|----|----|-----|
| 1 | Scene 1 + end of Scene 3 | "The joy plant is a stinking cucumber." | "The joy plant was, almost certainly, a stinking cucumber." | The body says "most likely" and Böck says "highly probably". The cold open shouldn't be more certain than the evidence. |
| 2 | Scene 3, One | "Proto-cuneiform, the earliest writing anywhere" | "Proto-cuneiform, among the earliest writing anywhere" | Egypt's Abydos tomb U-j labels are argued to be as early or earlier. |
| 3 | Scene 3, One | "Nobody wrote anything down about anything." | "In Mesopotamia, nobody was writing anything down yet." | Same reason as #2. |
| 4 | Scene 3, One (lower third) | "Earliest writing: proto-cuneiform, c. 3350–3300 BC" | "Earliest Mesopotamian writing: proto-cuneiform, c. 3350–3300 BC" | Same reason as #2. |
| 5 | Scene 3, Four (heading) | "The plant is probably a cucumber." | "The plant is probably a bitter gourd." | Colocynth is *Citrullus*, the watermelon genus, not a cucumber. |
| 6 | Scene 3, Four | "It's a desert gourd that was used as a violent purgative." | "It's a desert gourd, botanically a cousin of the watermelon, that was used as a violent purgative." | Same reason as #5. It also sets up the "cucumber" hook, which comes from the ancient word ("foul cucurbit"), not the botany. |
| 7 | Scene 8, Abydos | "500 years older than the pyramids and 150 miles away" | "roughly 450 years older than the Giza pyramids and about 270 miles away" | Giza (29.98°N) to Abydos (26.18°N) is about 430 km (270 mi) in a straight line. The brewery is Narmer era, c. 3000 BC, and Giza is c. 2560 BC. The Step Pyramid is only about 330 years later, so name Giza. |
| 8 | Scene 8 (visual) | "dotted line runs 150 miles south" | "dotted line runs about 270 miles south" | Same reason as #7. |
| 9 | Scene 9 | Personal aside | **Cut from this version** | Personal material isn't approved for the channel yet. Scene 10 moves to about 17:30 and the outro to about 18:45. Remove "17:30 Why a man like me checks Assyriology" from the description chapters. |
| 10 | Title option 2 | "The 'Joy Plant' Was a Cucumber" | Suggest "The 'Joy Plant' Was Probably a Cucumber" | Same reason as #1. It's your call: packaging, not a factual error. |
